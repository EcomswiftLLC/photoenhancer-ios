Common questions

How do I enhance a photo?
  1. On Home, tap a tool, then choose a photo from Photos or Files, or try a sample.
  2. Pick the settings, such as 4x for Upscale, and wait for the preview.
  3. Compare with the before and after view.
  4. Tap Save to Photos, Save to Files or Share.
The result still looks soft

Try Unblur for shake, then Sharpen with a higher strength. Very heavy blur, or a photo that was out of focus, can only improve a little: detail the camera never captured cannot be brought back. Starting from the original file, not a screenshot or a message attachment, gives the best result.

8x says the photo was reduced first

To keep your device responsive, results are limited to 8192 pixels on the long side and 48 megapixels. A large photo is reduced before the 8x pass so the result fits. Use 4x if you want to keep the original size as the starting point.

Processing is slow or stops

AI upscaling is heavy work, especially at 8x on older devices. Keep the app open until it finishes. If the device runs low on memory, Clarivo stops safely and tells you; try a smaller size or close other apps.

Why am I asked to watch an ad?

Ads pay for Clarivo. Before you save or share, the app offers a short video; a batch needs one ad, and saves in the next 2 minutes need none. If no ad is available, saving goes ahead for free.

I want to change my ad or tracking choice

Open Settings in the app and use Privacy choices where it appears. You can also change tracking in iOS Settings under Privacy and Security, then Tracking.

I saw an ad that seems wrong

Write to us with roughly when you saw it and what it showed. A screenshot helps. We block ad categories and advertisers through Google AdMob where we can.
