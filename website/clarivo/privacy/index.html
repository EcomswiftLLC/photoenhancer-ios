Privacy Policy

App: Photo Enhancer HD: Clarivo ("Clarivo")

Company: Ecom Swift LLC, 30 N Gould St #37906, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States

Contact: support@ecomswiftllc.com

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what Clarivo does with information. The short version: your photos are processed on your device and are never uploaded, and the only outside party that receives any data is the advertising service that pays for the app.

1. The short version

  • Clarivo has no account and no sign-in.
  • Your photos are enhanced, compared and exported on your device. We do not upload them and we cannot see them.
  • Clarivo is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device and ad data to show and measure ads. You are asked for your consent where the law requires it.
  • We do not run our own analytics, and there are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.
  • Clarivo itself builds no profile of you. Tracking can happen only through the ads: if you allow tracking when iOS asks, Google's advertising SDK may use your advertising identifier to show and measure ads. If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", it does not. See sections 4 and 5.

2. Photos that stay on your device

You choose which photos to open. Clarivo reads them through the iOS photo picker or the Files app, so it does not need access to your photo library. You can also start from one of the sample photos built into the app. When you save a result to your photo library, Clarivo asks for add-only permission, which lets it add new pictures and does not let it read the rest of your library.

All processing happens on your device: upscaling, unblur, sharpening, noise reduction, portrait enhancement, colorizing, old-photo restoration, auto fix, batch processing and export to JPG, PNG or HEIC. The AI models that do this work are built into the app and run with Apple's Core ML on your device. Finding faces for portrait enhancement uses Apple's Vision framework on your device. Your photos are not sent to Ecom Swift LLC, to Google, or to any server or AI service, and they are not used to train any model.

Photo metadata (such as the date, camera details and location stored in a photo) is read only to keep it in, or remove it from, the file you export, as you choose in the export options. It is never sent anywhere.

Recent results are kept in the app's own storage on your device so you can find them again. They are removed automatically after 7 days, and you can clear them at any time. They are never sent anywhere.

When you save or share a result, Clarivo gives the file to your photo library, the Files app or the iOS share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice. We do not receive it.

3. Information we receive

From the app: none. We do not receive your photos, your identity or your usage of the app.

From you, if you write to us: if you email support@ecomswiftllc.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and to keep a record of the request, and delete it when it is no longer needed. Please do not send photos unless we ask for one to look into a problem.

From the website: the Clarivo website pages (ecomswiftllc.github.io/clarivo/) are static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. They use no cookies of their own, no analytics and no advertising scripts. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are downloaded. GitHub may keep standard server logs for security and operation.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Clarivo is free and supported by ads shown through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The app shows a banner ad on the Home screen and, before you save or share a finished photo, offers one rewarded video ad. You always see your finished photo and the before/after comparison before any ad. There are no other ads, and no ad covers the photo you are working on.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and use, according to Google:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking, and other device and app identifiers
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • information about ads you see and interact with
  • device, operating system and app information and diagnostic information such as crashes and performance

Your photos are never shared with Google or any advertiser. We do not receive the advertising data and we do not combine it with anything we hold. Google's use of the data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and on the page "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Some US state privacy laws treat advertising of this kind as "sharing" or "targeted advertising". Clarivo does not sell your photos. You can limit ad personalisation as described in the next section.

5. Your choices about ads and tracking

  • Consent form (EEA, UK and other regions where required). On first launch a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform may appear. You can accept, decline or manage your choices, and reopen it later in Settings, in the Privacy choices row.
  • Tracking prompt. After the consent step, iOS may ask whether Clarivo may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies (App Tracking Transparency). If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads will be less personalised. The app works the same either way.
  • iOS settings. You can change the tracking choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and reset your advertising identifier there.
  • Ads cannot be switched off inside the app, because ads are how Clarivo is paid for. If ads cannot load for you (for example offline, or because of your consent choice), no banner appears and saving goes ahead without an ad.
  • Report an ad. If you see an ad that is offensive or misleading, write to support@ecomswiftllc.com with roughly when you saw it and what it showed. We block ad categories and advertisers through Google AdMob where we can.

6. Rewarded video and saving

Before you save or share a result, the app offers a short rewarded video. A whole batch needs one ad, and after an ad, further saves within 2 minutes do not show another one. If no ad is available, the save goes ahead without one. The app remembers that an ad was watched only on your device, for those 2 minutes. This record contains no personal information and is not sent anywhere.

7. Deleting your data

  • Clarivo has no account. Deleting the app removes everything it stores, including recent results.
  • Inside the app you can clear recent results at any time.
  • Photos you saved to your library and files you exported or shared are outside the app; you manage them where you saved or sent them.
  • Your device backup (for example iCloud Backup) may contain a copy of the app's data according to your Apple settings.
  • We hold no personal data about you from your use of the app, so there is nothing on our side to delete. For advertising data held by Google, use Google's controls (https://myadcenter.google.com).

8. Children

Clarivo is a general photo tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the local age of digital consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to support@ecomswiftllc.com and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, California and other US states), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port or restrict the use of personal information, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal information from your use of the app, there is usually nothing for us to provide, but you can write to support@ecomswiftllc.com about any request and we will respond within the time the law requires. For data held by Google, exercise your rights with Google. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

Your photos and recent results are protected by the security of your device and by iOS app storage protections. We recommend a device passcode. You are responsible for the files you export and share.

11. International transfers

Google and its partners may process advertising data in the United States and other countries. Where required, they rely on legal transfer mechanisms described in Google's policies.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date, and where a change is significant we will say so in the app's release notes.
