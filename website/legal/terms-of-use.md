Clarivo is an app for iPhone and iPad that enhances photos on your device. It can upscale photos 2x, 4x or 8x, reduce blur and camera shake, sharpen, reduce noise, sharpen faces in portraits, add colour to black-and-white photos, reduce scratches, dust and fading in old photos, and balance light and colour. You can compare the result with the original, process several photos at once, and export JPG, PNG or HEIC files.

## 5. What enhancement can and cannot do

Clarivo uses image-processing algorithms and AI models that run on your device. Upscaling, unblur, noise reduction and portrait enhancement make photos look sharper and cleaner by predicting plausible fine texture; they cannot recover detail that the camera never captured, and heavy blur may improve only a little. Colorizing produces an estimate of natural colours, not the true historical colours. Results depend on the photo you start from and can contain artefacts, so check every result before you rely on it, publish it or use it as evidence of anything.
