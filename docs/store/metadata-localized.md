(enhance, sharpen, upscale; no "recover lost detail"; colours are an estimate), the privacy line, the ad model stated

promo 170). Have a native speaker review before publishing. Keywords must not repeat words already in that locale's
name or subtitle. The descriptions are shorter than en-US on purpose; extend them if a translator is available.
