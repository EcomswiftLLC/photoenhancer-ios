Clarivo enhances photos on the device: AI upscale (2x, 4x, 8x), unblur, denoise, portrait sharpening, colorize, old-photo restore and auto fix. There is no account, no login and no demo credentials. Everything works on first launch and offline (the AI models are bundled in the app; nothing is downloaded or uploaded).

How to test:
1. Launch the app and answer the privacy (consent) and tracking prompts either way.
2. On Home, tap "Try a sample" and pick one of the built-in sample photos (for example "Small building photo" for Upscale, "Shaken street sign" for Unblur, "Street scene, 1864" for Restore and Colorize), or choose any photo from your library with the photo picker.
3. Choose a tool and tap the action button. A progress bar shows the work; the result appears with a before/after slider (drag it, pinch to zoom).
4. Tap Save to Photos, Save to Files or Share. A prompt offers a short rewarded video ad (Google AdMob); after it, the save goes through. If no ad is available, saving still works for free. Within 2 minutes after an ad, further saves do not show another ad.

Ads: one AdMob banner on the Home screen only, and the rewarded ad before saving or sharing (the result is always shown before the ad). There are no in-app purchases. The Google UMP consent form and the App Tracking Transparency prompt are shown on first launch; ads still work (non-personalised) if tracking is declined.

Permissions: only "add to photo library" when saving. Opening photos uses PHPicker, which needs no permission.

Privacy: photos are processed on the device with Core ML and are never uploaded. The sample photos are licensed from Pexels (Pexels License) and The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access (CC0); four samples are deliberately degraded copies made by us to demonstrate the tools.
