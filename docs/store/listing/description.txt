Make your photos sharper, cleaner and larger. Clarivo upscales, unblurs, removes noise, colorizes black-and-white photos and restores faded prints, right on your iPhone or iPad. Your photos are processed on your device and never uploaded. It is free and there is no account.

AI UPSCALE 2x, 4x AND 8x
- Enlarge small or cropped photos with an on-device AI model that adds fine, natural-looking texture as it scales
- 8x works in two passes up to 8192 pixels on the long side; Clarivo tells you when a photo had to be reduced first
- Large photos are processed in tiles, so even big files stay smooth on your device

UNBLUR AND SHARPEN
- Unblur reduces motion blur and camera shake with an AI deblur model
- Sharpen with a strength slider for photos that are only a little soft
- Honest note: heavy blur can improve, but detail that the camera never captured cannot be brought back

REMOVE NOISE
- Clean up grain from low light and high ISO, with a strength slider that blends with the original

PORTRAIT
- Sharpens faces it finds in the photo and blends them back smoothly. It enhances what is there; it does not invent or change faces

COLORIZE BLACK-AND-WHITE PHOTOS
- Adds natural-looking colour to old black-and-white photos with an AI colorize model, while keeping the full resolution of the original. Colours are an estimate, not a historical record

RESTORE OLD PHOTOS
- Reduce scratches and dust, lift fading and remove the yellow cast of old prints

AUTO FIX
- One tap to balance light and colour, with a strength slider

COMPARE BEFORE AND AFTER
- Drag the slider, pinch to zoom on both sides at once, or touch and hold to see the original

BATCH
- Enhance many photos with the same settings, with progress for each photo and a cancel button

EXPORT YOUR WAY
- JPG, PNG or HEIC, original size, 2x, 4x, 8x or a custom size, and a quality slider
- Keep or remove photo metadata, including location
- Save to Photos, save to Files or share. No watermark

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Every photo is processed on your device. Clarivo never uploads your photos, has no account and needs no photo library access to open a photo: you choose each photo in Apple's picker. It only asks for permission to add photos when you save.

FREE, WITH ADS
Clarivo is free. It shows a small banner on the home screen and a short video ad before you save or share a result; you always see your finished photo first. A whole batch needs one ad. If no ad is available, you can save for free. Ads are shown by Google AdMob; your photos are never shared with them.

Works on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later.
